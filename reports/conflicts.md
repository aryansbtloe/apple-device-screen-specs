# Source conflicts and judgement calls

Checked 2026-10-27 (IST). In every case the dataset keeps the value from the highest-ranked allowed source: Apple Tech Specs, then the Apple HIG, then Apple developer docs. Wikipedia is used only as a cross-check. Third-party sites are noted here but never used as a source.

| # | Devices | Conflict | What the dataset does |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | iPhone 12 mini, iPhone 13 mini | The HIG (snapshot 2026-07-02) lists **360×780 pt @3x**. The site tool uses **375×812 pt @3x** with downsampling. | **Settled by Xcode 27.1 Simulator (iOS 27.0 runtime), 2026-10-27:** `UIScreen.main.bounds` = **375×812 pt**, `scale` 3, `nativeScale` **2.88**, `nativeBounds` 1080×2340 px. Both device types render a 1125×2436 canvas (`canvasWidth/Height` in capabilities.plist) and downsample it to the 1080×2340 panel (1080/375 = 2.88). 360×780 is just the panel divided by 3, which UIKit never reports at the default Standard display zoom. The run did not test Display Zoom. The dataset now uses 375×812 pt, `native_scale` 2.88, and CSS 375×812 @3 (derived with the same points = CSS px rule as every other device; not checked in Safari). Evidence: sources/simulator/raw/iPhone-1{2,3}-mini.json. |
| 2 | All devices from 2026 on | On 2026-09-09 Apple removed the device-dimension tables from the live HIG Layout page. Points and scale come from the Wayback snapshot 20260702060104 of `layout.json`. | The 2026 iPhones (18 Pro / Pro Max, 17e) use points derived from an identical-panel HIG row and are flagged in `provenance`. iPhone Duo (inner display) and iPad mini (A20 Pro) have points = null. Xcode 27.1 Simulator confirms 18 Pro (402×874), 18 Pro Max (440×956), 17e (390×844) and iPad 10th gen (820×1180). iPhone 5, 5s and 5c have no Simulator device type, so their derived 320×568 stays unconfirmed. |
| 3 | iPad 10th gen, iPad Air 4/5 vs iPad (A16), iPad Air 11" M2–M4 | Apple markets the same 1640×2360 panel as **10.9"** on older pages and **11"** on newer ones. The pixel rectangle measures 10.86". iPad Pro 11" (M4/M5) measures 11.1" against a marketed 11". | `diagonal_in` keeps Apple's marketed figure per page, and `diagonal_rect_in` stores the computed rectangle. The validator allows a 3% gap from the marketed diagonal (Apple measures rounded corners and the marketing figure is rounded). |
| 4 | iPhone Duo | Third-party sites disagree on the inner display's points (ios-resolution.com 626×890, appetiteui.com 669×951). In Xcode 27.1 (iOS 27.1 runtime) the Duo device type declares two integrated displays. The cover display `LCD` is 1398×2034 px @3x and matches Apple. The inner display `LCD-1` is **2007×2853 px** @3x (hdpi 460), against Apple's **1878×2670 px at 430 ppi**. 2007/3 × 2853/3 = 669×951, which is appetiteui's figure. At launch `UIScreen.screens` exposed only the cover (466×678 pt, nativeScale 3, `_displayCornerRadius` 59; its plist corners are UL/LL 8 pt and UR/LR 59 pt). | Cover display: points 466×678 @3x (measured). Inner display: points stay **null**, because the Simulator never exposed it and its declared panel does not match Apple's pixels. The cover's safe-area insets looked anomalous (identical in portrait and landscape, right 84 pt, 2 pt status bar), so they are kept only as raw data under `secondary_screens[].simulator_measurement`. |
| 5 | Apple Watch Series 10 | Apple contradicts itself on display area. support.apple.com **en-us**/121202 gives **1196 / 970 sq mm** (46/42 mm). **en-gb**/121202 gives **1220 / 989 sq mm**. Later en-gb pages (125093, 148589) give 970 for 42 mm. | Keeps en-us: 1196 / 970. |
| 6 | Apple Watch Series 10–12 | Apple states 326 ppi. ios-resolution.com states 330. | Apple's 326. |
| 7 | MacBook Pro (Liquid Retina XDR) | Apple's spec pages say "Liquid Retina XDR" and never say "mini-LED". | `display_type` = mini-LED, with provenance "Wikipedia cross-check". This is the only display-type value not taken from Apple wording. |
| 8 | iPhone 4 / 4S, iPod touch 4th gen | Apple's 326 ppi against 329.7 ppi computed from the rounded 3.5" diagonal. | Keeps 326. The validator allows a 2% tolerance against `diagonal_rect_in`. |
| 9 | iPad 7th gen | Apple's spec page has a typo ("pixel per inch"). | The value is parsed correctly (264 ppi). |
| 10 | iPad mini (A20 Pro) | Listed at apple.com/ipad-mini/specs/ but has no support.apple.com Tech Specs page and no HIG row. Wikipedia marks it as upcoming. | `status` = "announced". The source is apple.com. Points are null. |
| 11 | bestlatestapps.com tool | It doesn't list iPhone 17e or iPhone Duo, or models older than 6s (6/6 Plus, SE 1st gen, 5-series and earlier). The 8 Plus row shows 1080×1920 (the physical panel) next to 414×736 @3x (rendered at 1242×2208). | The dataset has all of these. The site table should be regenerated from `data/devices.json`. |
| 12 | Apple Watch Series 1–6: release year | The Apple spec pages have no "Year introduced". | Taken from Wikipedia and noted in `provenance.release_year`. |

| 13 | iPhone 6s Plus, 7 Plus, 8 Plus | The Simulator device types declare a 1242×2208 @3x display with `hdpi` 461. The `ScreenDimensionsCapability` key gives 1080×1920 at pitch 326 instead. | No conflict on pixels: Apple's 1080×1920 matches `ScreenDimensionsCapability`, and 1242×2208 is the render canvas. The Simulator `hdpi` 461 is the canvas density, not Apple's 401 ppi, so Simulator DPI is never used for `ppi`. |
| 14 | Simulator DPI vs Apple PPI | Simulator `hdpi` matches Apple's ppi on every device except the Plus models above and the Duo (cover 460 vs inner 430). For 18 older Watches where Apple states no ppi, the Simulator says 326. | `ppi` keeps Apple's numbers only. Simulator DPI values are recorded in sources/simulator/ and not merged. |
| 15 | iPhone 12 / 12 Pro / 12 Pro Max corner radius | The static `DeviceCornerRadius` is 47 / 47 / 53. The runtime private `_displayCornerRadius` is 47.33 / 47.33 / 53.33. Every other booted type agrees to 0.01 pt. | Uses the runtime value. All `corner_radius_pt` values are UNOFFICIAL. Apple publishes none. |
| 16 | iPad safe areas | In iPadOS 27's default windowing mode, `requestGeometryUpdate` fails with "The current windowing mode does not allow for programmatic changes to interface orientation". A retry with `UIRequiresFullScreen` did rotate, but the insets were asymmetric (landscapeLeft bottom 10, landscapeRight top 10). Portrait was 32/0/20/0 on every iPad, home-button models included. | iPad `landscape` = null. Portrait insets are recorded with a provenance note on the windowing mode. Raw data from the retry: sources/simulator/experiments/. |
| 17 | Apple Vision Pro | The Simulator device type is a 3840×2160 @2x virtual window (also a 2732×2048 variant), not the per-eye panel. | Not used for `resolution_px`. Mentioned in `notes` only. |

Machine-readable versions are in `reports/build_report.json` (`conflicts`, `warnings`) and in each device's `notes` and `provenance`.
